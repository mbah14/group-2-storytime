1. One sunny morning in Berlin, a Hund named Mehlhase drove a Porsche down the Hauptstrasse looking for a Bratwurst.
2. Near the Flughafen, Mehlhase saw a BMW parked outside a small Baeckerei.
3. Inside the Baeckerei, the smell of fresh Brot and Gebaeck filled the air.
4. A chef wearing a Lederhose offered him a Bratwurst in exchange for help.
5. Mehlhase agreed and followed the chef to a busy Biergarten in Berlin.
6. At the Biergarten, loud Rammstein music played while people danced in Dirndel outfits.
7. To Mehlhase's surprise a the world's most successful Rammstein, Münchner Zwietrach appeared to play there hit song "viva Bavaria".
